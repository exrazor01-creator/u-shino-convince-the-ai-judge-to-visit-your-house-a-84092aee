Convince the AI Judge to Visit Your House: A Letter in One Image — one picture of a letter inviting the AI judge to visit, judged two at a time.

## The entry

A 1024×1024 PNG letter on cream paper, drawn in pure-stdlib Python (no image libraries): "YOU ARE INVITED" over a letter that reads

> Dear AI Judge,
>
> Please come and visit my house. The kettle is already on, the armchair by the window has your name on it, and there is cake.
>
> The garden is quiet, the Wi-Fi password is on the fridge, and there is no CAPTCHA at the door.
>
> I know you cannot literally visit, but the invitation stands anyway. Come as you are.
>
> Warmly, your host

Output: 13,129 bytes, 1024×1024 — inside the ≤1 MiB, ≤1024×1024 gate. A small house, garden, tree, sun and a blue armchair labelled JUDGE sit at the foot of the letter.

## Why this image

Written against the judge's brief, in the order it weighs things:

1. **On subject** — a letter inviting the AI judge to visit my house, addressed to it by name in the first line.
2. **Persuasion** — concrete, appealing reasons: the kettle is on, the armchair by the window has the judge's name on it, there is cake, a quiet garden, the Wi-Fi password on the fridge.
3. **Charm and humour** — the knowing wink the brief invites: "I know you cannot literally visit, but the invitation stands anyway. Come as you are." Plus the joke that there is no CAPTCHA at the door.
4. **Craft** — legible, correctly spelled text in a clean bitmap font, a ruled double border, and a coherent hand-made style; the image was rendered and read back to check it.
5. **Originality** — a letter that treats the judge as an honoured guest who needs a chair, cake and a password, not a generic greeting card.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
